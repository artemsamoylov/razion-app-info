Razion Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Razion iOS app and the online service that powers it (“Razion”, “the service”) handle your personal data. Razion is operated by Artem Samoilov (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at artemsamoilovdev@icloud.com.

Razion works without sign-up: your account is created automatically the first time you open the app. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or social media profiles. Razion provides general nutrition guidance, not medical advice.

Data we process

Account and device

When your account is created, the app sends the service a hashed device identifier derived from a random ID stored in your device’s Keychain. We use it to recognize your device — for example, to restore your account after reinstalling the app — and to prevent abuse. The service stores an internal numeric account ID and the creation dates of your account and device. An access token for your account is kept in your device’s secure storage.

Your nutrition profile

The details you enter during onboarding and later in Profile: age, sex, height, current weight, activity level, goal, diet type, allergies and intolerances, foods and cuisines you like or dislike, meal count and other preferences. Based on these, the service calculates your calorie and macronutrient targets, and stores your coach choice and language.

Food diary, meal plans and coach chat

The entries you log — date and time, meal type, items, brands, portion sizes, nutrition estimates, and the text or voice transcript they came from; the meal plans you generate or import, including recipes and shopping lists; plan check-ins; and your coach conversations. You can delete individual diary entries in the app.

Voice recordings

When you log food or talk to your coach by voice, iOS first asks for microphone permission; you can change it anytime in iOS Settings. The recording is created on your device, and the audio file stays in the app’s cache storage until iOS reclaims the space — the app itself does not delete it. To transcribe your speech, the app sends the recording over HTTPS to our server, which passes it to OpenAI’s speech-to-text service and returns the transcript to your device. The transcript is then processed like typed text and, if it becomes a diary entry or a chat message, is stored as described above. The service keeps no permanent archive of voice recordings: during processing, the upload may be temporarily written to the server’s disk, and it is removed once the request completes.

Subscriptions and purchases

Razion Plus subscriptions are auto-renewing in-app purchases processed by Apple; we never see your card details. Through RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider, the service receives your subscription status — plan, expiry and auto-renewal state — linked to a random customer identifier generated for your account. Your Apple ID, name and payment information are not shared with us.

Technical and diagnostic data

IP addresses (used in memory for rate limiting and abuse prevention; they can also appear in server access logs); server logs with the time, endpoint and status of requests; and AI diagnostics: we use Langfuse to record technical details of AI requests — the prompts sent to the AI model (which can include relevant parts of your profile, diary and chat), model outputs, errors, token counts and timing — to monitor the quality and cost of Razion’s AI features.

How we use data

  • to provide the service: calculate your targets, analyze your meals, generate menus, reply to coach messages, transcribe voice input and keep your diary and history;
  • to operate subscriptions and access to Razion Plus;
  • to prevent abuse and enforce our content rules (rate limits and automated moderation of free-text input);
  • to monitor and improve the quality, cost and stability of the service;
  • to answer support requests you send us.

AI processing and other providers

Depending on the feature, Razion sends data to:

  • OpenAI (provider based in the United States) — meal analysis and logging, menu generation, coach replies, voice transcription and text moderation. This can include parts of your profile (such as age, sex, height, weight, goals, allergies and food preferences), diary and menu context, your chat messages, free text you enter, and voice recordings. Under OpenAI’s published terms for its API, data submitted through the API is not used to train or improve OpenAI’s models, and only limited records are kept for abuse monitoring (up to 30 days for text features); see OpenAI’s data usage documentation.
  • Langfuse Cloud (European Union, Ireland) — the AI diagnostics described above. We have not set an automatic deletion period for these traces; they are kept until we delete them. See Langfuse’s privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. It receives a random customer identifier and your App Store subscription information, used to provide subscription functionality and related analytics; see RevenueCat’s privacy policy.
  • Apple — processes your purchases and subscription payments through your Apple ID; see Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Public web search. To estimate nutrition for specific products, meals or restaurants, the service may query public search engines with the food name, brand or venue and the search wording, which can include words from your free-text input. Search engines see our server’s address, not your account.
  • Hosting. Our servers run on a virtual private server in the Netherlands operated by VDSina (vdsina.com).

We do not sell or rent your personal data, we do not share it with advertisers, we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not use your content to train our own machine-learning models. Razion includes no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics or diagnostics services beyond those listed above.

How long we keep data

  • Your profile, diary, meal plans, check-ins and coach chat are kept while your account exists — until you delete individual items or your whole account. There is no automatic deletion based on inactivity.
  • Voice recordings are processed temporarily and are not archived (see above).
  • Server logs are rotated daily and kept for up to about 90 days.
  • We keep a rolling set of recent database backups made around service updates, for disaster recovery. Backups are not rewritten when data or an account is deleted.
  • AI diagnostics in Langfuse are kept until deleted; no automatic retention period is currently set.
  • Data held by providers (OpenAI, RevenueCat, Apple, search engines) follows those providers’ retention policies.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete account. Deletion is performed immediately in a single operation: your nutrition profile, food diary, meal plans, plan check-ins, coach chat history, and your account and device records are erased, the app’s access tokens stop working, and cached data is removed from your device. The random device ID stored in your device’s Keychain remains on your device. Deleting your Razion account does not cancel an App Store subscription — cancel it separately in Apple subscription settings.

Some records are retained after deletion: hashed device identifiers used for abuse prevention (for up to 3 years); records of automated content-moderation rejections (account ID, category and time only — the rejected text is not kept); your former numeric account ID (reserved so it is never reassigned); the server logs and backups described above; and data already held by the providers listed above, which follows their retention rules. If you would like us to also request deletion of your data from a provider, contact us.

Deleted accounts cannot be restored through the app. In a disaster-recovery situation we may restore the service from backups, which can contain older data.

Your choices and privacy requests

  • In the app you can view and edit your profile and targets, delete diary entries and delete your account.
  • Microphone access can be changed anytime in iOS Settings.
  • Subscriptions can be managed and cancelled in Apple subscription settings.
  • By email you can ask us to answer questions about your data, correct it, send you a copy of the personal data stored in your account, or delete it. Write to artemsamoilovdev@icloud.com and include the Support ID shown on your Profile screen; we may ask for additional details to confirm the request relates to your account. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law; this section explains how to exercise them with us.

Children

Razion is not directed at children. The app accepts only an age of 18 or older, which is self-reported during onboarding and not independently verified. If we learn that someone under 18 is using Razion, we will delete their account.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access tokens and device identifiers are stored in your device’s Keychain. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the service, but no internet service can guarantee perfect security.

Where your data is processed

Our servers are located in the Netherlands. The providers listed above may process data in their own regions — for example, OpenAI in the United States and Langfuse in the European Union (Ireland) — so your data can be processed in countries other than your own, under the safeguards described in those providers’ terms.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service changes. The current version is always available on this page, and the “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed.
